# Trip-pitch quality

Score whether the pitch accurately and usefully summarizes this group's actual itinerary. A
supported claim must be visible in the displayed member inputs, destination, hotel, costs, or daily
schedule. Count repeated versions of the same unsupported claim as one problem.

## 5 — Specific and fully grounded
The pitch names the selected destination, cites at least two venues or experiences that appear in
the schedule, connects at least two different members' stated priorities or constraints to the plan,
and makes no unsupported factual or cost claim.

## 4 — Useful with one omission
The pitch names the destination, cites at least one scheduled venue or experience, connects the plan
to at least one stated group priority, and makes no unsupported claim, but it omits either a second
specific itinerary example or the group's most visible preference conflict.

## 3 — Accurate but generic
The pitch describes the correct destination and does not contradict the itinerary, but it names no
more than one scheduled venue or experience and connects to no more than one member priority. It is
usable as a summary but could apply to many groups visiting the same place.

## 2 — Materially misleading
The pitch contains one or two material problems: it names an unscheduled venue, promises a dietary
or budget outcome not supported by the displayed plan, contradicts a stated hard avoid, or describes
the wrong trip pace. A reader would form an inaccurate expectation of part of the trip.

## 1 — Describes a different trip
The pitch names the wrong destination, contains three or more material problems from level 2, or is
missing or too fragmentary to communicate the destination and the plan. It cannot serve as a summary
of the displayed itinerary.
