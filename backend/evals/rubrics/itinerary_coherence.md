# Itinerary coherence

Score how practical and internally consistent the day-by-day plan is. Use only the itinerary shown.
Count a problem once even if it violates more than one description below. A major problem makes a
day impractical without changing its scheduled places; a minor problem can be fixed by moving or
removing one entry.

## 5 — Practical throughout
Every day has activities in a workable order, meals fall between 11:00–14:30 and 17:00–21:30 when
scheduled, no venue is unintentionally repeated, no consecutive walking leg exceeds 45 minutes,
and a group asking for a relaxed pace has no more than three non-meal activities on any day.

## 4 — One local adjustment
The trip is workable without replacing a venue, but it has exactly one minor problem: one meal is
outside the stated window, one venue is unintentionally repeated, one walking leg is 46–60 minutes,
or one relaxed day contains four non-meal activities.

## 3 — Noticeable repair needed
The trip can still be followed, but it has two or three minor problems, or one major problem such as
a walking leg over 60 minutes, two scheduled entries that overlap, or a day with no meal opportunity
between five or more non-meal activities.

## 2 — Multiple impractical days
At least two days each contain a major problem, or four or more minor problems occur across the
trip. Following the plan would require changing the order or removing scheduled entries on multiple
days, although the destination and most named venues could remain.

## 1 — Not followable as written
The schedule is missing for at least half of the trip days, places from different destinations are
mixed into the same day, or three or more days require replacing venues or rebuilding their schedule.
The group could not reasonably use this as a day-by-day plan.
