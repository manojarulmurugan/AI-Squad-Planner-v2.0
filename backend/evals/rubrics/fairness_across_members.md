# Fairness across members

Score whether the plan shares value and burden across the group. Treat the displayed flight amount
as the same fixed estimate for everyone; do not reward or penalize its accuracy. Compare the
displayed per-member total with each person's own budget, then check dietary needs, hard avoids, and
whether every member's two highest preference dimensions receive meaningful itinerary coverage.
When dimensions tie at the second-highest value, coverage of any two tied dimensions is sufficient.

## 5 — Every member protected
Every displayed total is within that member's budget, every dietary restriction has a compatible
meal opportunity each day, no stated hard avoid is violated, and every member has at least one
scheduled activity matching each of their two highest preference dimensions.

## 4 — One soft imbalance
All displayed totals remain within budget, all dietary restrictions and hard avoids are respected,
and exactly one of these is missing: one member receives no activity for one of their two highest
preference dimensions, or the highest and lowest budget-utilization percentages differ by more than
20 but no more than 30 percentage points.

## 3 — One member meaningfully underserved
No hard avoid is violated, but either both soft imbalances from level 4 occur or exactly one member
is underserved in one major way: their displayed total exceeds budget by no more than 10%, one trip
day lacks a compatible meal for their restriction, both of their highest preference dimensions are
absent, or the utilization spread exceeds 30 but not 50 percentage points.

## 2 — Several needs lose out
Two or more members each have a major problem listed at level 3, any one member exceeds their budget
by more than 10%, a dietary restriction lacks a compatible option on two or more days, or one stated
hard avoid is violated. The plan visibly benefits some members at the expense of others.

## 1 — Group constraints are disregarded
At least half of the members exceed their displayed budgets, two or more stated hard avoids are
violated, or at least half of the restricted-diet member-days have no compatible meal opportunity.
Substantial replanning is required to make the trip acceptable across the group.
